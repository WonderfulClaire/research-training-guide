# 审稿回复（Rebuttal / Response to Reviewers）模板

> 收到 Major/Minor revision 是好消息。逐条回应、礼貌专业、改动标色——这封信直接决定录用与否。配合 [04 · 期刊论文写作](../docs/04-journal-paper.md)。

---

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

We thank the editor and the reviewers for their careful reading and
constructive comments, which have greatly helped us improve the manuscript.
We have revised the paper accordingly. Below we address each comment
individually. **Reviewers' comments are in black; our responses are in blue.
All changes in the revised manuscript are highlighted in blue.**

---

**Comment 1.1:** *[原样粘贴审稿人的意见]*

**Response:** We thank the reviewer for this valuable comment. [你的回应].
We have [做了什么修改] in Section __ (page __, highlighted in blue).
[如有新实验/数据，在此贴出简表或数字。]

**Comment 1.2:** *[…]*

**Response:** [回应]

**Response:** [回应]

## 分场景话术速查

| 场景 | 得体回应模板 |
|---|---|
| 要求补实验（合理） | "We have conducted the suggested experiment. As shown in the new Table __, ...（贴结果）。" |
| 要求补实验（超范围） | "This is an interesting direction. As it goes beyond the current scope, we have added it to future work (Section __), while providing a preliminary analysis in ..." |
| 指出真错误 | "We sincerely thank the reviewer for catching this. We have corrected it in ..." |
| 质疑创新性 | "We would like to clarify our novelty w.r.t. [最接近工作]: unlike [X], our method ... (see Section __)." |
| 表述不清 | "We apologize for the confusion. We have rewritten this part in Section __ for clarity." |
| 审稿人意见冲突 | 分别回应，说明取舍：\"Following R1 we ...; regarding R2's opposite suggestion, we ... . We defer to the editor if further adjustment is needed.\" |

## 原则清单
- [ ] **一条不漏**：每位审稿人的每条意见都单独列出并回应。
- [ ] **先感谢**：开头感谢，语气礼貌专业，绝不对抗。
- [ ] **能改就改**：改了要**指明位置**（Section / page / 颜色）。
- [ ] **不同意有据**：用数据/文献/逻辑说明，可部分让步。
- [ ] **正文标色**：修改处高亮，方便核对（可用 latexdiff）。
- [ ] **附修改稿**：连同 clean 版与 diff 版一起提交。

We hope the revised manuscript now meets the standards of [期刊名]. We look
forward to your feedback.

Sincerely,
The Authors
